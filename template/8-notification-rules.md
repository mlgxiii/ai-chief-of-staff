# My notification rules

<!-- What interrupts me now vs what waits for a brief. -->

**Interrupt me now:** urgent, high-impact, blocking, security or financial risk.
-

**Daily brief:** important but non-urgent.
-

**Weekly review:** patterns, drift, longer-term decisions.
-

**Stay silent:** everything else.
